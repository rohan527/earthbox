🌱 Getting Started

Congratulations on your new terrarium! Here's what to do first:

  1. Find the perfect spot with bright, indirect light
  2. Let your terrarium acclimate for 24 hours before watering
  3. Check for condensation - a little is good, too much means it needs air
  4. Remove the lid (if closed) for a few hours if you see excessive moisture

💧 Watering Guidelines

Closed Terrariums

  • Water sparingly - once a month or less
  • The enclosed environment creates its own water cycle
  • Soil should be slightly moist, never soggy
  • If condensation covers more than 1/3 of the glass, reduce watering

Open Terrariums

  • Water when soil feels dry to the touch (usually every 1-2 weeks)
  • Spray lightly with a misting bottle
  • Succulents and cacti need even less water
  • Ensure good drainage to prevent root rot
💡 Pro Tip: Use distilled or filtered water to prevent mineral buildup. Tap water can leave white spots on the glass.

☀️ Light Requirements

Proper lighting is crucial for healthy plants:

  • Ideal Location: Near a window with filtered light
  • Avoid: Direct sunlight (can overheat and burn plants)
  • Signs of Too Much Light: Brown or yellow leaves, dried out soil
  • Signs of Too Little Light: Leggy growth, pale leaves, slow growth
  • Artificial Light: LED grow lights work great for 10-12 hours daily

✂️ Maintenance & Pruning

Regular Maintenance

  • Remove dead or yellowing leaves promptly
  • Trim plants that grow too large with clean scissors
  • Wipe the inside of the glass with a soft cloth if it gets dirty
  • Check for pests monthly (though rare in closed systems)

When to Prune

  • Plants touching the glass or lid
  • Overgrowth blocking light to other plants
  • Leggy or unhealthy stems
  • To maintain your desired aesthetic

🐛 Troubleshooting Common Issues

Excessive Condensation

Solution: Remove lid for 2-4 hours, then replace. Repeat until condensation is minimal.

Mold or Fungus

Solution: Remove affected areas immediately. Increase air circulation. Reduce watering.

Yellow or Brown Leaves

Solution: Check watering schedule. Ensure proper light. Remove dead material.

Slow or No Growth

Solution: Increase light exposure. Check if plants need more space or nutrients.

Pests

Solution: Remove pests manually. Isolate terrarium. Consider replacing affected plants.

🌿 Plant-Specific Care

Ferns

Love humidity. Keep moist. Enjoy indirect light. Perfect for closed terrariums.

Moss

Needs constant moisture. Thrives in shade. Spray frequently in open terrariums.

Succulents

Prefer dry conditions. Need bright light. Water sparingly. Best in open terrariums.

Ivy

Fast grower. Needs regular pruning. Moderate water. Adaptable to various light conditions.

Fittonias (Nerve Plant)

Loves humidity. Needs consistent moisture. Prefers shade. Dramatic when thirsty but recovers quickly.

📅 Seasonal Care

Spring/Summer

  • More active growth period
  • May need slightly more water
  • Watch for too much direct sun as days get longer
  • Good time for pruning and maintenance

Fall/Winter

  • Growth slows down
  • Reduce watering frequency
  • May need supplemental lighting
  • Keep away from heating vents

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take? +
Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days. We also offer expedited shipping (2-3 days) and express shipping (1-2 days) at checkout. All terrariums are carefully packaged to ensure they arrive in perfect condition.
Do you ship to my area? +
We ship to all 50 US states and many international locations. During checkout, enter your zip code to see available shipping options and estimated delivery times for your area.
What if my terrarium arrives damaged? +
We take great care in packaging, but if your terrarium arrives damaged, please contact us within 48 hours with photos. We'll arrange for a replacement or full refund at no additional cost to you.
Can I customize my terrarium beyond the builder? +
Absolutely! While our builder offers many options, we're happy to work with you on fully custom designs. Contact us with your ideas, and we'll create a custom quote for your unique vision.
How often do I need to water my terrarium? +
It depends on the type! Closed terrariums may need water once a month or less, while open terrariums typically need watering every 1-2 weeks. Check out our detailed Care Guide for specific instructions.
Do you offer corporate or bulk orders? +
Yes! We offer special pricing for bulk orders and corporate gifts. Terrariums make excellent employee gifts, event favors, and office decor. Contact us for a custom quote.
Are your terrariums pet-safe? +
Most of our plants are non-toxic, but we recommend keeping terrariums out of reach of curious pets. If you have specific concerns, contact us and we can recommend pet-friendly plant options.
What's your return policy? +
We offer a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your terrarium, contact us for a return or exchange. Living plants must be returned in their original condition within 7 days.
